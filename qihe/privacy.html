Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

Effective date / 生效日期: November 13, 2026

English

Qihe is an independent app for viewing health records and trends. Contact the developer at qq927884591@gmail.com.

Health data and permissions

With your permission, Qihe reads relevant records from Apple Health to display sleep, activity, body stress references and related trends, and to provide optional reminders. Qihe does not write health records to Apple Health. You can change access in the Health app or your device settings.

Storage and sharing

Qihe processes health information on your devices. It stores app settings and derived health caches locally on your iPhone, and sends a compact snapshot and preferences to your paired Apple Watch using Apple’s device connectivity. Qihe does not send your health records to a developer-operated server or sell them. System backups may include app data, subject to your device and backup settings. Apple Health and Apple services have their own privacy practices.

Retention and deletion

Qihe keeps bounded caches for recent summaries and personal comparisons. In Qihe’s data management settings, you can clear the cache or erase app data; the paired watch is cleared after it reconnects. These actions do not delete records in Apple Health or existing system backups. Manage those separately through Apple’s settings. Removing Health access stops future authorized reads but does not itself erase previously cached data.

Support and this website

If you email us, we use the information you choose to send to respond to your request. Please avoid sending health records or sensitive personal information. This website is hosted by GitHub Pages; GitHub may process technical information under its own privacy policy.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The updated policy will be posted here.

栖和（Qihe）是一款由个人开发的健康记录与趋势查看应用。联系邮箱：qq927884591@gmail.com。

健康数据与授权

经你授权，栖和读取 Apple 健康中的相关记录，用于展示睡眠、活动、身体压力参考和相关趋势，并提供可选提醒。栖和不会向 Apple 健康写入健康记录。你可以在健康 App 或设备设置中更改授权。

存储与共享

栖和在你的设备上处理健康信息，在 iPhone 本地保存应用设置和派生的健康缓存，并通过 Apple 的设备连接功能向配对的 Apple Watch 同步精简摘要和偏好。栖和不会将你的健康记录发送到开发者运营的服务器，也不会出售这些记录。系统备份可能包含应用数据，具体取决于设备和备份设置。Apple 健康及 Apple 服务适用各自的隐私政策。

保留与删除

栖和为近期摘要和个人比较保留有范围限制的缓存。你可以在应用的数据管理设置中清除缓存或抹掉应用数据；配对手表重新连接后会清除相应数据。这些操作不会删除 Apple 健康中的记录或已有系统备份，请在 Apple 的设置中分别管理。撤销健康访问会停止后续授权读取，但不会自动删除之前的缓存。

政策更新
